## Rules

- A founder-run demo is not discovery, and it is not activation.
- Disposable or test data does not count as use.
- Record the conversations that went badly; they change the product more than
  the ones that went well.
- Nothing here is permission to contact anyone. Outreach needs explicit
  approval (`ATT-HANDOFF-02`, `blocked`).
