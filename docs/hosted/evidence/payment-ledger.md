# Payment ledger (G39)

Prepared 2026-09-07 by W11. **Empty. Nothing accepted, nothing invoiced,
nothing collected.**

> **Do not fabricate.** No agreement, invoice number, amount or payment may be
> recorded that does not exist. Do not convert an accepted offer into revenue,
> and do not report any MRR: a pilot payment is learning evidence, not
> recurring revenue.

## Three separate states

These are **not** stages of one number. A team can be in state 1 and never
reach 2. Reporting them as one figure is the failure this ledger exists to
prevent.

| State | Means | Evidence required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Accepted** | The team said yes to a specific offer, in writing, with a date | The written acceptance |
| **Invoiced** | An invoice was actually issued to them | The invoice, with number and date |
| **Collected** | Money arrived | The bank or processor record |

## Arithmetic, stated so it is not misread

Under the plan's own assumptions: five paying teams at $25/month is
$125/month; a fully spent $300 budget leaves −$175 before any salary. Two
paying teams is $50/month. That is why pilot payment is evidence that someone
will pay at all, not a viable margin. Do not present it as traction.

## Rules

- No assumed recurring revenue, ever.
- An accepted offer with no invoice is one line in the "Accepted" column and
  nothing else.
- A collected payment must be traceable to a bank or processor record, not to
  a promise.
- Making an offer, issuing an invoice or taking payment needs explicit
  approval (`ATT-HANDOFF-02`, `blocked`).
